# Replay context readability refinement

Product Owner feedback: 14 October2026. The owner found race context hard to read,
requested inspiration from Formula Timer's live-timing presentation, reported missing
and stale portraits, and asked why forecasts begin at lap33.

This authorizes visual hierarchy/readability refinement and portrait fixes within the
approved historical Replay Explorer information/interaction scope. Timing receives
more width and priority; map remains available and shares comparison selection.
Preserve all approved tabs, explicit missing states, subject/clock independence,
full distributions, outcome isolation and data authority. It does not authorize new
live/telemetry domains, extrapolated predictions, model tuning or deployment.

Early support failures are a frozen-baseline limitation; explain it without changing
published forecasts. Research details remain in research artifacts. Presentation
uses the public reference for hierarchy, not code, photographic assets or data.
